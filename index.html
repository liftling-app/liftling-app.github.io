Health noteLiftling is a training log, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or a qualified coach before starting a new program, and stop if something hurts.

Privacy policy

Liftling is made to keep your training private. In short: we do not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your workouts stay on your phone

Everything you enter in Liftling (workouts, sets, routines, custom exercises and settings) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted too. Your iPhone may include it in its own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your Apple settings.

Backups and sharing

When you export a backup or share a workout image, the iPhone share sheet opens and you choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, WhatsApp or Instagram). What happens there is covered by that service’s own privacy policy.

Internet use

Liftling works without internet. At most once a day it asks Apple’s App Store whether a newer version is available. This request contains only the app’s ID, not anything about you or your workouts.

Notifications

The “Rest is over” alert is created on your phone. It only appears if you allow notifications, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Tips

Tips are optional one-time purchases handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, card or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to these purchases.

Children

Liftling is not aimed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal data, it holds no data about children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. If we ever start collecting any data, we will ask you first.
